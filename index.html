Victoria Ezechukwu: Webinar Funnel System
Upwork coach helping freelancers grow their income online
Step 1 · The Problem

Victoria was closing every client manually through WhatsApp. Every sale meant answering the same questions, chasing payments, and following up one by one. It wasn't scalable, and it was eating time she didn't have.

Step 2 · The Ad

I wrote and designed the ad creative that ran cold traffic straight into the funnel, positioning the offer around earning in dollars from a phone or laptop.

Step 3 · Registration

Anyone who clicked landed on a registration page built to capture just enough information: name, email, and preferred session time.

Step 4 · The Webinar

Behind the scenes, I configured the automated webinar in WebinarJam so it could run on a schedule without Victoria needing to show up live for every session.

Step 5 · Payment

After the webinar, viewers landed on a direct checkout page for the Digital Skills Training, removing every extra step between interest and payment.

Step 6 · Follow-up

For anyone who registered but didn't buy immediately, an automated email sequence followed up to bring them back to the offer.

Step 6 · Follow-up (cont.)

Every email in the sequence was tested end to end before launch to confirm it delivered properly and read well on mobile.

Steadvolt Energy: Pay-on-Delivery Sales Funnel
Landing page and order system for a CCTV camera seller in Nigeria
Step 1 · The Problem

Steadvolt Energy was running ads straight into WhatsApp. Every buyer had to be chatted with, convinced, and closed by hand, one conversation at a time. That is slow, people slip away between messages, and it doesn't scale.

ptz.steadvoltenergy.com Open live page ↗
Step 2 · The Landing Page

I built a dedicated landing page on his own domain. Scroll through the live page above. Ordering is switched off in this preview, so nothing you tap here sends a real order.

Step 3 · The Order Flow

A buyer fills the order form once. The details land in the owner's inbox, he passes them to the delivery company, and the buyer pays when the order arrives. Pay on delivery matters here because many Nigerian buyers won't send money before they see the product. Meta Pixel tracking is installed so every visit and order can be measured.
